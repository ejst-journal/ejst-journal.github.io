Ion Implantation Induced Sputtering in 2D MXene (Ti3C2Tx): A Computational Study

Muhamad Iqbal1; Tahir Parveez1; Shahbaz Afzal2; Fawad Ali3; Ateeq ul Rehman4

Archive home / Volume 5 · Issue 2

Year: 2025   Volume: 5   Issue: 2   Pages: 168-177

DOI: 10.48309/ejst.2025.474150.1177

Abstract

MXene is a flexible two-dimensional material that is made of transition metal carbides and nitrides. It is of high significance due to its remarkable features, which include effective ion intercalation and good electrical conductivity. Ti3C2Tx is distinct among MXenes because of its unusual structure of alternating titanium and carbon layers. New developments have looked at using ion implantation to change the MXene characteristics, taking advantage of its capacity to cause structural changes and controlled doping. In this novel study, the Stopping and Range of Ions in Matter (SRIM) binary code has been utilized to understand the ion implantation induced sputtering mechanism in 2D MXene, crucial for understanding structural and functional changes in MXene. The noble ions such as He, Ne, Ar, Kr, and Xe have been incident with the angles varying from 0 to 90º. The study findings open up new avenues for customized applications in energy storage and other fields, with ion implantation being identified as a potential means of further developing MXene-based materials.

Keywords
