Synthesis of Thiazoles Using Amberlyst Resin as a Reusable Heterogeneous Catalyst

Popat M. Jadhav; Anand V. Dhirbassi; Sushama S. Kauthale; Sunil U. Tekale

Archive home / Issue 4

Pages: 294-298   DOI: 10.48309/ejst.2026.581826.1318

Abstract

Thiazole is an important five-membered heterocyclic ring containing nitrogen and sulfur atoms, playing a crucial role in synthetic and medicinal chemistry. The ring has a unique structural ability for target binding and metabolic stability, which has resulted in its utility in several commercially marketed drugs. In the present work, a rapid and efficient method has been developed for the synthesis of substituted thiazole derivatives from various substituted phenacyl bromides and thiourea in refluxing ethanol. A series of 2-amino thiazoles was synthesized in good to excellent yield of the corresponding products using Amberlyst resin as a heterogeneous catalyst. The developed protocol offers excellent yield involving a reusable catalyst and an easy work- up procedure, making the method more feasible, economical, and environmentally sustainable for the synthesis of thiazoles

Keywords

Amberlyst resin, Aminothiazole, Phenacyl bromide, Thiourea
