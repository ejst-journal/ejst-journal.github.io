Ardabil Province's Breast Cancer Epidemiological Study, 2003-2022: An Update Study

Poone Zamani; Firouz Amani

Archive home / Issue 3
Volume: 5 (2025), Issue 3Pages: 243-251DOI: 10.48309/ejst.2025.488301.1192

Abstract

Breast cancer (BC) is the most common cancer in women and the second most common cancer globally. In 2018, there were two million new cases recorded. Investigating BC's epidemiological trends in the Ardabil Province from 2003 to 2022 was the goal of this study. We used cancer registries statistics which stratified by age group and gender to estimate the BC incidence rates in this cross-sectional descriptive study. We used statistical techniques in SPSS version 24 to examine the gathered data. The patients were 49.6±13.8 years old on average. 98.03% of the patients were female, and the majority (54.9%) were between the ages of 40 and 60. With an incidence rate of roughly 10.41 cases per 100,000, the majority of BC cases were reported in 2011, with Ardabil City accounting for 71.5% of all cases. Between 2003 and 2022, the incidence rate of BC increased by 87.6%, from 4.13 per 100,000 to 7.75. Over the years under study, the annual percentage change was 3.62%. The frequency of BC grew between 2003 and 2011, and then declined between 2011 and 2013, before rising once more between 2014 and 2022. The results of study showed that breast cancer incidence rate in Ardabil's population were increased during the study years and it is recommended to done new future epidemiological studies about the risk factors for BC and its cause.

Keywords
