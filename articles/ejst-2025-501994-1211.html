Abstract

Introduction oday, the development of solid- state welding methods for titanium alloys is considered [1]. One of these popular methods is Resistance Welding (RW) methods [2]. RBW (RBW) is one of the popular methods for creating the connection of different metals. This method is considered as a method with high speed and successful connection quality in ferrous metals such as steel [3,4]. Sharifi- Tabar also conducted research on the joining of non-ferrous alloys, including Ti-6A-4V. The results of this study showed that the strength of the joined sample was up to 94% of the strength of the base alloy, which was due to the creation of some pores during welding [5].
