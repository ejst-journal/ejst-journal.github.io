Nutritional and Phytochemical Analysis of Spinach Leaf Aqueous Extract: A Comprehensive Study on Proximate Composition, Minerals, Vitamins, and Antioxidant Activity

Ibrahim Mohammed Ibrahim; Ndukaire Victoria Oluchukwu; Abubakar Salisu

Archive home / Issue 3
Volume: 5 (2025), Issue 3Pages: 302-311DOI: 10.48309/ejst.2025.495035.1199

Abstract

Vegetable Leaf plays an essential role in food and diet conservation. Spinach, in particular, is recognized for its rich content of vitamins, minerals, and phenolic compounds, making it a superior source of these nutrients. Compared to staple foods such as grains, vegetables leafy contain greater amounts of essential minerals like iron and calcium. This study examines the nutritional and phytochemical analysis of spinach leaf aqueous extract as a comprehensive study on proximate composition, minerals, vitamins, and antioxidant activity of spinach leaf Aqueous extract. The tannins, flavonoids, phenol, glycosides, saponins, and alkaloids are present after the phytochemical screening results. The moisture, ash, Crude fat, crude fiber, crude protein, carbohydrate, and total Energy content was obtained as follows; 8.66%, 14.29%, 2.43%, 8.46%, 22.75%, 43.40%, and 286.47%, respectively, as the results of the proximate analysis. The composition of minerals showed results on the average, potassium concentration recorded was the highest (4249.35 mg/100 g), while Zinc was observed to be the lowest with a concentration range of 54.82 mg/100 g. The antioxidant activities revealed the highest percent inhibition (86.12%) in 0.5um concentration and the lowest percent inhibition 44.09% in 0.03125 um concentration also; vitamins analysis results reveal the highest quantity (198.44 mg/100 g) in Vitamins A and then the lowest (0.74 mg/100g) in Vit. B3. This study shows that vegetables in particular spinach might be a worthy supplement for some diets and elements analyzed.
