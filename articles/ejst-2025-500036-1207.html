Introduction troke is the second leading cause of death in people over 50 years of age in the world, with a global incidence of stroke of over 13.7 million per year and a mortality rate of 86.52 per 100,000 population between 1990 and 2016. Ischemic stroke is the most common type of stroke. Ischemic stroke is an episode of neurological dysfunction that occurs due to vascular stenosis, spasm, and occlusion [1,2]. The pathophysiology of ischemic stroke begins with inadequate blood supply to a focal area of brain tissue.
