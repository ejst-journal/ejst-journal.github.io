Ischemic stroke is the most common type of stroke. Ischemic stroke is an episode of neurological dysfunction that occurs due to vascular stenosis, spasm, and occlusion [1,2]. The pathophysiology of ischemic stroke begins with inadequate blood supply to a focal area of brain tissue.
