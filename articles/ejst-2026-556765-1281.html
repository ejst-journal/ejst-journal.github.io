Revolutionizing Type 2 Diabetes Care: Scientific Journey of Fiber Rich Cookies Made from Green Unripe Jackfruit and Sorghum Flour

Rakesh U. Shelke; Anjali D. Kshirsagar; Tanishk D. Khairnar; Payal V. Patil; Eknath D. Ahire; Khemchand R. Surana

Archive home / Issue 1

Abstract

Artocarpus heterophyllus (jackfruit) used in the formulation is the best way to control or maintain blood glucose levels. In small intestine, the high levels of fiber in jackfruit slow digestion by interacting with sugar, cholesterol, fat, and decrease their absorption in the body. The formulated supplement mainly contains gluten free sorghum flour and date syrup which is rich source of vitamin B complex along with seeds that have antioxidant properties. These ingredients also help to improve the nutritional efficacy of the product, prevent heart diseases, and maintain blood glucose levels. Together, these components minimize the quick absorption of sugar and offer a balance of macronutrients, making your cookies a healthier choice for diabetics. Evaluated parameters include general appearance, qualitative tests, patient acceptability, HbA1c tests (blood glucose level tests), and more. Additionally, these cookies exhibit a lower glycemic impact compared to traditional ones, making them a more desirable option. The inclusion of green unripe jackfruit in the formulation of cookies results in low glycemic index, whichcould potentially, provide health benefits for individuals suffering from degenerative diseases such as diabetes mellitus. The incorporation of functional ingredients like green unripe jackfruit, dates, and sorghum in the development of novel products holds great promise for the food industry. From this study it can be concluded that, this approach not only helps reduce waste, but also presents opportunities for cost reduction in the production of baked goods. The use of unripe jackfruit in cookie manufacturing addresses both environmental concerns and economic efficiency in the baking industry
