Numerical Investigation of the Mechanical Behavior of Polyethylene Beams Containing Recycled Polyethylene

Yasaman Ghasemi; Omid Ashkani

Archive home / Issue 4

Pages: 342-351   DOI: 10.48309/ejst.2026.583381.1329

Abstract

There are many types of materials, one of which is plastic materials, and among the common plastic materials is polyethylene. These materials play an important role in everyday use because they have good properties such as strength, flexibility, lightness, low cost, and better thermal properties and electrical insulation, which can be used in many industries. To enhance the performance of polymers, they are often combined with other materials to form polymer composites consisting of two or more phases, at least one of which is polymeric. By blending a polymer with another polymer or reinforcing material, unique combinations of mechanical and physical properties can be achieved. In recent years, the increasing consumption of plastics has intensified concerns regarding resource management, environmental sustainability, and plastic waste generation, leading to greater interest in recycling and reintroducing these materials into the production cycle. Characteristics such as environmental compatibility, low density, durability, favorable life-cycle performance, and cost-effectiveness have contributed to the widespread use of plastics and polymer composites in various engineering applications. In this study, virgin polyethylene was used as the matrix material and reinforced with recycled polyethylene at different weight ratios, including a specimen composed entirely of recycled polyethylene. The objective of this research was to determine the optimal recycled polyethylene content for practical applications while maintaining suitable mechanical performance. Based on the simulation and mechanical analysis results, the composite containing 30% recycled polyethylene exhibited the best balance between mechanical strength, dimensional stability, and reduced consumption of virgin material
