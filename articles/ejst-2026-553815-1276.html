The Exploratory Use of HDPE Waste Plastic for Thermal Insulation Applications

Sunday A. Osemeahon; Jlliya I. Nkafamiya; Joshua A. Gidigbi; Ayodele Akinterinwa; Akinda Markus; Henry L. Barnabas

Archive home / Issue 2

Pages: 151-167   DOI: 10.48309/ejst.2026.553815.1276

Abstract

The study explored the possibility of replacing polyester polyol with HDPEPO in the development of rigid polyurethane foam as a thermal insulator. The HDPE waste plastics were prepared and pyrolysed at different temperatures (450, 475 and 500 ᵒC) to yield high-density polyethylene pyrolysis oil (HDPEPO). The effect of different pyrolysis temperatures on important physicochemical parameters, such as density, yield, viscosity, iodine value and flash point, revealed that while density increases and flash point decreases with increasing pyrolysis temperature. Fourier-transform infrared spectroscopy (FTIR) and gas chromatography–mass spectrometry (GC–MS) analyses of the pyrolysis oil confirmed the presence of hydroxyl compounds in the HDPEPO, and their concentration decreased as the pyrolysis temperature increased, as higher temperatures promote the dehydration of alcohols to alkenes and water. The HDPEPO was used to formulate a polyurethane (PU) thermal insulator. The FTIR analysis carried out on the PU thermal insulator formulated revealed the presence of urethane linkage; however, it failed to rise, hence the need to modify HDPEPO with PEO to form HDPEPO/PEO blend. The effect of an increase in HDPEPO concentration on the crucial parameters (hydroxyl value, acid value, viscosity, and density) of HDPEPO/PEO blend revealed that the properties at 10% HDPE fall within the acceptable range. The characterisation of the HDPE-PU thermal insulator formulated using this blend is within the acceptable range when compared with the standard. The thermal insulator showed improvement in water absorptivity and thermal conductivity when compared with conventional thermal insulators. The percentage of HDPEPO utilised can be increased when PEO with a high hydroxyl value is deployed in the formation of HDPEPO/PEO blend

Keywords

Waste HDPE, Pyrolysis, Waste management, Polyol, Rigid polyurethane foam
