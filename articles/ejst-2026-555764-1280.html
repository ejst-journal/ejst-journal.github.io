Optimisation of HDPE-PU Thermal Insulation: Effect of Processing Parameters on Insulation Properties

Joshua A. Gidigbi; Sunday A. Osemeahon; Abdulrasaq B. Abubakar; Galo Y. Sara; Martins E. Aisuedion; Kazhigila Thomas; Bashir Hamman

Archive home / Issue 2

Pages: 182-199   DOI: 10.48309/ejst.2026.555764.1280

Abstract

Thermal insulation material is crucial for energy maximisation and conservation. The HDPE waste plastics were pyrolysed at 475 ᵒC. The physicochemical parameters analysis such as density, acid value, viscosity, iodine value, pour point, flash point and cloud point were carried out on the HDPE pyrolytic oil (HDPEPO). The physicochemical results agree with literature. The 10% HDPEPO was copolymerised with 90% polyester polyol (PEO) to produce HDPEPO/PEO blend. The blend was used in HDPE-PU thermal insulator production. The effect of processing parameters such as surfactant, catalyst and blowing agent was assessed on important parameters of HDPE-PU thermal insulator. The new formulation conditions established from the optimisation of processing parameters were used to formulate new HDPE-PU thermal insulator and compare with conventional PU thermal insulator and acceptable values for thermal insulatord. The properties of novel HDPE-PU thermal insulator align with and acceptable values, and show better thermal insulation and high compressive strength when compared with conventional PU thermal insulators

Keywords

Thermal Insulation, Energy Conservation, Processing Parameters, Waste Plastics, Copolymerisation
