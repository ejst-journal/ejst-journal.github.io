Recent Advances in Synthesis, Characterization, and Applications of Schiff Base Ligands: A Comprehensive Review

Milad Mahjoobizadeh; Vahidreza Darugar; Fatemeh Khodabakhshi

Archive home / Issue 4

Pages: 352-373   DOI: 10.48309/ejst.2026.588264.1334

Abstract

Schiff base ligands have attracted considerable attention in modern chemistry due to their facile synthesis, structural diversity, and wide range of applications in coordination chemistry, medicinal chemistry, catalysis, and materials science. These compounds are commonly prepared through condensation reactions between primary amines and carbonyl compounds, producing the characteristic azomethine (–C=N–) functional group responsible for their versatile chemical behavior. This review provides a comprehensive overview of recent advances in synthesis and characterization of new Schiff base ligands. Different synthetic approaches, including conventional reflux methods, green synthesis strategies, microwave-assisted techniques, and solvent-free procedures, are discussed with emphasis on their advantages and limitations. Furthermore, important characterization techniques such as FTIR, NMR, UV–Vis spectroscopy, mass spectrometry, X- ray diffraction, and thermal analysis are summarized to highlight their role in structural confirmation and property evaluation. The biological, catalytic, environmental, and material applications of Schiff base ligands and their metal complexes are also reviewed. Finally, current challenges and future perspectives regarding sustainable synthesis, advanced functional materials, and biomedical applications are discussed. This review highlights the continuing importance of Schiff base chemistry and its potential contribution to the development of multifunctional compounds for future technological and scientific applications. The review also briefly highlights the emerging electrochemical and bioanalytical applications of Schiff base ligands, which represent a growing area of interest

Keywords
