Evaluation of Physicochemical and Metallic Properties of Covered and Uncovered Groundwater Wells in Ado-Ekiti, Nigeria

Julius Gbenga Omosebi; Hawawu Alake Olorunoje; Mary Oluwaferanmi Ayodele

Archive home / Issue 4
Volume: 5 (2025), Issue 4Pages: 385-392DOI: 10.48309/ejst.2025.515578.1242

Abstract

This study presents the physicochemical and heavy metal analyses of water samples obtained from covered and uncovered wells in the selected areas of Ureje, Damola, and Erifun. The results of the physicochemical analysis, including parameters such as color (colourless), pH (5.42-6.50), electrical conductivity (169-238 μS/cm), total dissolved solids (145-202 mg/L), temperature (26.70-27.00 0C), total hardness (28.00-56.00 mg/L), calcium hardness (11.22-22.45 mg/L), magnesium hardness (16.78-33.55 mg/L), sulphate (8.90-34.00 mg/L), nitrate (1.20 to 4.86 mg/L), chloride (78.10 -127.80 mg/L), and alkalinity (200-560 mg/L). The findings suggest variations in these parameters between covered and uncovered wells, emphasizing potential differences in water quality and composition. Insights into the concentrations of heavy metals, includes iron (Fe) (0.133 - 0.387 ppm), lead (Pb) (0.022 - 261 ppm), copper (Cu) (0.013 - 0.234 ppm), chromium (Cr) (ND - 0.021 ppm), and cadmium (Cd), with the "not detected" (ND) notation indicating absence in all the samples. The data highlights potential heavy metal contamination in the water sources, particularly in the uncovered wells, raising concerns about the safety and quality of the water supply in the studied locations. These findings emphasize the importance of regular monitoring and appropriate management strategies to ensure the provision of clean and safe drinking water to the communities in these areas.

Keywords

Ground water, Covered well, Uncovered well, Heavy metals, Physicochemical properties
