Abstract

Introduction lthough water is essential to life, its availability does not always align with human demand. Everywhere in the world, governments strive to provide their people access to clean, abundant water. Accessing both groundwater and surface water resources is an integral part of this effort. Human and natural activities significantly threaten water bodies globally. Groundwater quality is at risk due to increasing anthropogenic pressures and environmental changes, which also make it more difficult to evaluate the groundwater's current and future geographical distribution [1]. Wells can become contaminated due to activities happening near their openings.
