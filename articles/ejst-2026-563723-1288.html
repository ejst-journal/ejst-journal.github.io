The Optical and Photoluminescence Features of GdNiSe Material Synthesized Hydrothermally

Fawad Ali; Blessing Odia Ogo; Osama Tariq Satti; Jamil Ur Rehman Siddiqi; Imosobomeh L. Ikhioya; Muhammad Numan Nawaz

Archive home / Issue 2

Pages: 200-211   DOI: 10.48309/ejst.2026.563723.1288

Abstract

In this study, the hydrothermal method was used to synthesize the gadolinium nickel selenide (GdNiSe) material. The materials' energy bandgaps ranged from 1.58 eV to 1.18 eV. Annealing the material led to a reduction in the energy band gap. The energy bandgap of GdNiSe was enhanced through the annealing process . The photoluminescence was carried out by exposing the materials to sunlight at different time intervals. The unannealed GdNiSe material was exposed to sunlight at 20-minute intervals, ranging from 0 to 140 minutes. The prominent peaks were identified within the wavelength range of 612 to 661 nm. X-ray diffraction (XRD) analysis of the GdNiSe material has shown that it crystallizes in a hexagonal crystal structure with space group P63/mmc. The lattice parameters of GdNiSe are a = 0.5875 nm and c = 1.0256 nm. The XRD patterns of GdNiSe material are affected by annealing. It impacts the intensity, position, and size of peaks, revealing details about the material's structure and microstructure. The FTIR spectra showed various peaks, such as 2,654, 1,869, 1,638, 1,105, and 935 cm-1. The GdNiSe material exhibits a strong covalent bond, leading to a short bond length and high bond strength.
