A Highly Validated RP-HPLC Method Development and Validation for Simultaneous Estimation of Vildagliptin and Dapagliflozin in Synthetic Mixture

Amit Kumar J. Vyas; Chetan Khandala1; Dhara U. Desai1; Ashvin V. Dudhrejiya1; Ashok B. Patel1; Ajay I. Patel; Sunny R. Shah2; Devang B. Sheth3

Archive home / Volume 5 · Issue 4

Year: 2025   Volume: 5   Issue: 4   Pages: 357-365

DOI: 10.48309/ejst.2025.512085.1225

Abstract

Vildagliptin and Dapagliflozin were quantitatively analysed using a diode array detector-based RP-HPLC technology that is specific, precise, accurate, robust, cost-effective, and reproducible. At 40 °C, the chromatogram was run through an Enable C18 column (15 cm × 4.6 mm, 5 µm) using a mobile phase that contained 20 mM ammonium acetate buffer {40: 60} and acetonitrile: methanol in the ratio (80: 20). At 220 nm, the solution was pushed through the column at a rate of 1 mL/min. The retention times for Dapagliflozin and Vildagliptin were 8.523 and 2.443 minutes, respectively. For Vildagliptin and Dapagliflozin, the calibration plots were created across the concentration ranges of 10-125 μg/ml and 1-12.5 μg/ml, respectively, with correlation coefficients of 0.9997 and 0.9992. According to the ICH standard, the developed technique was verified to conform to all validation parameters. This approach may be successfully used for routine drug analysis in both research laboratories and pharmaceutical companies.

Keywords

Vildagliptin, Dapagliflozin, RP-HPLC, Method, validation, ICH guideline (Q2R1)
