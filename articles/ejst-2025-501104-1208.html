UV Visible Spectrophotometric Method Development and Validation for Estimation of Mirabegron by First Order Spectrophotometry Method

Devaliya Dhaval; Chetan Khandala; Bharat S. Pithiya; Meet Jagad; Mehta Maulik; Devaliya Ankit; Rangani Darshan; Pathak Dharmil

Archive home / Issue 3
Volume: 5 (2025), Issue 3Pages: 312-321DOI: 10.48309/ejst.2025.501104.1208

Abstract

Mirabegron is a β3-adrenoceptor agonist used to treat overactive bladder by relaxing the detrusor muscle, increasing bladder capacity without affecting voiding contractions. To develop and validate a spectrophotometric method for the first-order derivative method of determining Mirabegron that is straightforward, accurate, specific, precise, and reasonably priced. Using water as a diluent, Mirabegron demonstrated absorbance at working wavelengths of 231 nm. The ICH Q2(R1) standard was used to verify this method. The 1-36 μg/mL concentration range showed linearity, with correlation values of linearly of Mirabegron 0.999 and 0.998, respectively. The accuracy ratings of Mirabegron ranged from 99.74 to 100.16%. The results demonstrated that the developed method is suitable for routine analysis of the Mirabegron determination, with LOD and LOQ of 0.625 μg/mL and 1.895 μg/mL, and this method is robust because RSD less than 2.0.

Keywords

Mirabegron, First-Order derivative, UV spectrophotometry, ICH guideline
